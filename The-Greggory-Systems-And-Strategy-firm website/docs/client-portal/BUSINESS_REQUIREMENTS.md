# Client Portal Business Requirements

## Must-have experience
- Show active engagements and their progress.
- Show invoices and payment status.
- Show actions and milestones completed by the company.
- Show documents and deliverables related to the client.
- Offer a safe messaging channel to the company.

## Future expansion
- Upload and download files directly.
- Add a payment history section.
- Add a live project timeline and milestone calendar.
- Surface contracts, reports, and signed approvals.
